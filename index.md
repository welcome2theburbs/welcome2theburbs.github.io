# Welcome 2 The 'Burbs

*A fan-written sequel to the 1980s classic* The 'Burbs.

Thirty-odd years after the Klopek incident, Mayfield Place has gone quiet. Ricky is back to clear out his late parents' house. Ray's son Dave is "just keeping an eye on" his. The only original neighbor left is a very old Lt. Rumsfield, with a very young caregiver. And something has moved into the house everyone swore they'd never look at again.
